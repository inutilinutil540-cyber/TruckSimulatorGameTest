THE JOB

Pick a contract from dispatch. Your rig is already coupled — follow the amber beacon and the navigation map to the destination depot.

Stop inside the amber delivery zone. Your trailer will be unloaded and the fee paid into your balance. Late deliveries lose part of the fee; damage costs you at the workshop.

GOOD TO KNOW
  • Stay off the grass — your speed and grip drop sharply.
  • Speed cameras issue fines. Watch the limit signs.
  • Rain reduces grip and lengthens your stopping distance.
  • Refuel and repair in the Garage, or be towed at a cost.
